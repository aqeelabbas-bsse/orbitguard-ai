Architecture
From live TLE data to a signed-off recommendation

Every screen in OrbitGuard AI is bound to the real output of this pipeline — two deterministic simulation phases, two grounded LLM agent calls, and a generated report. Nothing here is placeholder data.

Problem

280 conjunctions were flagged from live-tracked orbital data in a single 96-hour window, across just 240 objects in two populations. Reviewing every close approach by hand doesn't scale as tracked debris grows.

Approach

A deterministic pipeline screens and simulates every close approach, then hands the highest-risk events to RAG-grounded watsonx.ai agents that assess severity and recommend the smallest maneuver that adequately reduces risk.

Impact

Every recommendation ships with its full reasoning trace and a generated incident report — an explainable answer operators can act on, not a black box.
